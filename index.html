Research-informed capability

Decision science
for real business choices

Our approach draws on Value-Driven Decision Modeling (VDDM), developed through doctoral research on how organizations define objectives, evaluate alternatives, and make consequential decisions. We use these methods where they help clarify tradeoffs and connect choices to measurable business value.

01
Stakeholder mapping

Distinguish who holds decision authority from who bears the consequences. Surface the hidden value conflicts driving escalation and stall.

02
Value elicitation

Use laddering techniques to move past stated goals. Uncover Core value, Utility (how we measure it), and Salience (how much it matters, to whom).

03
Objective engineering

Convert abstract values into specific, measurable objectives. Make implicit tradeoffs explicit and shared. Leadership stops negotiating and starts deciding.

04
Network quantification

Build a unified Objective Network that identifies the highest-impact pathways to the primary business goal — the map leadership has been missing.

05
Alternative generation

Score operational strategies against the quantified value network. Decisions are now defensible — to the board, to investors, to the team.

Decision frameworks are one input, not a required package. When technology helps put the right information and workflows into practice, Northlight can architect the solution, oversee implementation, or deliver defined technical components.

Client experience · Business & technology transformation

Modernizing the operations behind a hospitality business.

Connecting business strategy, operational redesign and technology to support a more integrated, data-informed organization.

The challenge

A hospitality business relied on separate systems for bookings, customer coordination, financial reporting, marketing and fulfillment.

Manual processes, spreadsheets and disconnected applications created operational complexity and made it difficult to bring financial and operational information together for management decisions.

The approach

Assess · Architect · Transform

Working as a fractional operations and technology executive, Northlight’s founder combined business and financial analysis, operational redesign, systems architecture and hands-on technical delivery.

The work addressed three connected priorities.

Operational efficiency

Predictive workforce planning, digital booking and customer coordination, streamlined service workflows.

Financial intelligence

Consolidated booking, accounting and payroll information; management dashboards; costing and profitability analysis.

Digital transformation

Customer-facing workflows, online ordering and fulfillment tools, and AI-enabled marketing analysis.

Selected results

  • A shared reporting foundationBrought booking, payroll and accounting information together to support management review of activity, costs and financial performance.
  • Digital customer coordinationIntroduced a portal supporting customer selections, approvals, deposits and synchronization with internal systems.
  • An additional ordering channelBuilt online ordering capabilities connected to preparation and fulfillment workflows.
  • Data-informed workforce planningDeveloped predictive models to support staffing decisions.
  • More connected workflowsLinked customer-facing processes with the internal information and tools needed to deliver services.

The Northlight difference

Business strategy informs the architecture. Technology supports the operating model. Executive leadership connects design with execution.
